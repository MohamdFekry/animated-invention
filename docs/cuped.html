Lesson 3

CUPED asks: better or worse than expected?

Some hours are naturally hard. CUPED compares each bucket with its own historical expectation before comparing treatment and control.

The key word

Residual = actual minus baseline.

If dinner peak usually takes 42 minutes and treatment takes 41, treatment was 1 minute better than expected.

Plain English

Do not compare an easy hour to a hard hour raw.

A quiet control hour at 23 minutes can look better than a dinner treatment hour at 41 minutes. But dinner may usually be 42 minutes, while quiet hours are usually 22 minutes.

When it helps

Use it when history predicts the metric.

CUPED helps when fleet, hour, weekday, or load history says a lot about what the bucket would normally look like.

Product lesson
